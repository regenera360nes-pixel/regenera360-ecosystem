## Integrated Sales Funnel Architecture

### Stage 1: Awareness (TOFU - Top of Funnel)
**Objective**: Generate 10,000 monthly visitors

**Channels**:
- SEO-optimized blog content (40+ articles)
- Social media presence (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube)
- Educational video content (20+ videos)
- Guest posts on health websites

**Metrics**:
- Website traffic: 10,000/month
- Social engagement rate: 5%
- Video views: 50,000/month

### Stage 2: Interest (MOFU - Middle of Funnel)
**Objective**: Convert 20% of visitors to leads

**Tactics**:
- Lead magnets (eBooks, guides, checklists)
- Monthly webinars on health topics
- Email nurture sequences (7-email series)
- Free assessment tools

**Metrics**:
- Email subscribers: 2,000/month
- Webinar attendees: 200/month
- Lead magnet downloads: 500/month

### Stage 3: Decision (MOFU)
**Objective**: Move 15% of leads to consultation

**Tactics**:
- Case studies and success stories
- Video testimonials from patients
- Virtual clinic tours
- Free initial consultation offers

**Metrics**:
- Consultation requests: 300/month
- Conversion rate to consultation: 15%
- Show rate for consultations: 80%

### Stage 4: Action (BOFU - Bottom of Funnel)
**Objective**: Convert 40% of consultations to bookings

**Tactics**:
- Online booking system (Calendly integration)
- Stripe payment processing
- Automated confirmation emails
- Pre-appointment preparation guides

**Metrics**:
- Bookings: 120/month
- Booking conversion rate: 40%
- Average revenue per booking: €500
- Total monthly revenue: €60,000

### Technology Stack Integration
- **CRM**: HubSpot (contact management, pipeline tracking)
- **Marketing Automation**: ActiveCampaign (email sequences, SMS)
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics 4 (behavior tracking, conversion optimization)
- **Payment Gateway**: Stripe (secure payments, subscriptions)
- **Booking System**: Calendly + Custom integration
- **Live Chat**: Drift (real-time support)
- **A/B Testing**: Optimizely
